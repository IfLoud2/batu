Frequently asked questions

FAQ

How much does a campaign cost?

The price depends on the location, the duration and the type of screen. We provide a quote on request, with no commitment.

Who creates the advertising shown?

You do. You send us your artwork and we display it.

How long can I advertise?

From the length of an event to a long-term presence, for example a full year.

Where are the screens?

In Belgium: covered venues, events and main roads. Tell us your area in the form.

Do you own the screens?

Yes. B&K Consulting owns its LED screens, fixed, modular or mobile.
